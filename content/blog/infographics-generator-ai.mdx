## How to Create an Infographic with AI: Step-by-Step

### Step 1: Define Your Goal
What message should the infographic communicate? Who is your audience? A clear goal produces better AI output.

### Step 2: Write Your Prompt
Be specific. Instead of "make an infographic about marketing," try:

> "Create an infographic showing the 5 stages of a content marketing funnel: Awareness, Interest, Consideration, Conversion, and Retention. Include metrics for each stage."

### Step 3: Generate and Review
Run the AI generation and review the output. Check that:
- The hierarchy is correct
- All key information is included
- The visual flow makes sense

### Step 4: Edit and Refine
Use the editor to adjust anything that needs tweaking. Move elements, change colors, update text, or ask the AI to regenerate specific sections.

### Step 5: Export and Share
Choose the right format for your use case:
- **PNG** for blog posts and presentations
- **SVG** for further editing or high-resolution prints
- **GIF** for social media and messaging
- **MP4** for video content and ads
